Vendor license supplements

librewolf 159.0.1-1:
LibreWolf-159.0.1-licenses.html is the license document shipped inside the exact installed browser build. It contains Mozilla and bundled-component notices. Source is available in the corresponding release source kit under Third-party Sources/LibreWolf.

libqt-jami 6.12.0-2:
Qt-and-QtWebEngine-license-notices.tar.gz contains the unmodified license/notice files extracted from the supplied Qt 6.12.0 and QtWebEngine 6.140.0-rc source archives, including Chromium and embedded third-party notices. Extract it to inspect the individual notices. Corresponding source, FFmpeg 9.0.1 and Jami contrib inputs are in Third-party Sources/Jami. These retain their individual license terms; they are not licensed by the original-project MIT license.

The standard Debian copyright files are absent from these two vendor packages. These supplemental documents preserve their upstream notices separately. GNU license texts are also installed under /usr/share/common-licenses.
